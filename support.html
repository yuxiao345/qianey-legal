钱伲（Qianey）· 记账这件事，我们帮你记好

需要帮助？
发邮件到 yuxiao.sky@me.com， 请附上你的 iOS 版本、App 版本，以及问题的截图，我们回复会快很多。

系统要求

  • iOS 18.0 或更高版本
  • 支持 iPhone 与 iPad
  • 需要登录 iCloud 才能同步与共享账本（不登录也能记账，只是数据不会跨设备同步）

我的数据存在哪里？会不会丢？

你的账目存在你自己的 iCloud 私有空间里，不在我们的服务器上——我们没有那样的服务器。 设备本地也会保留一份副本，所以断网也能记账。

详见隐私政策。

换了新手机，数据怎么回来？

  1. 在新手机上用同一个 Apple ID 登录 iCloud；
  2. 从 App Store 安装钱伲并打开；
  3. 数据会自动同步下来。账目多的话首次同步需要等一会儿，请保持联网。

数据不在我们手上，所以我们无法替你迁移——但只要你还在用同一个 Apple ID，它自己会回来。

怎么把账本共享给家人？

在账本设置里找到共享入口，按系统提示发出邀请即可。家人需要：

  • 登录自己的 Apple ID 与 iCloud；
  • 点开你发来的邀请链接，接受后即可在 App 里看到这本账。

几个人记的是同一本账，谁花的、谁垫的钱、AA 该给谁，都能对清楚。 想退出共享账本，在账本设置里选择退出即可，退出后你自己的数据仍然归你。

怎么删除我的数据？

  • 删一部分：在 App 内删除对应的交易、账本或附件；
  • 全删：删除 App，再到 iOS「设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud → 管理存储空间」里删掉钱伲的数据。

想先留个底，可以随时把账目导出成 CSV 或 JSON 文件（账本设置 → 数据导出）。

忘记 App 锁了 / 面容 ID 认不出来，打不开怎么办？

App 锁的开关保存在设备本地，账目在 iCloud。所以：

  1. 删除 App；
  2. 重新安装；
  3. 用同一个 Apple ID 登录后，账目会同步回来，App 锁会回到关闭状态，你可以重新设置。

没有任何人会因为忘记密码而失去自己的账目。

预算怎么开始做？

不用一上来就把所有分类填满。正确的用法是先记账：

  1. 正常记一两个月；
  2. 打开预算页，会看到「没进预算的开销」单独列出来，告诉你钱实际花在哪；
  3. 再决定哪些该管起来、每月给多少。

父分类设总额后，还可以往下给子分类分子限额。

能记外币吗？

可以。当一笔交易的币种和账本本位币不同时，App 会去取当时的汇率帮你折算。 正因为这一步要联网，这也是本 App 唯一的外部网络请求（详见隐私政策）。

能导出吗？导出成什么格式？

可以，随时导出，格式为 CSV 或 JSON，可自行带走、备份或用别的工具处理。

两台设备数据对不上 / 同步很慢

  • 确认两台设备都登录了同一个 Apple ID；
  • 确认「设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud」里钱伲的开关是打开的；
  • 保持网络连接，数据量大时首次同步需要几分钟；
  • 下拉刷新或重启 App 再看一次。

如果仍然对不上，来信附上截图，说明两台设备的型号与系统版本，我们来查。

Support

Qianey · We take care of the bookkeeping; you take care of the habit

Need help?
Email yuxiao.sky@me.com. Include your iOS version, app version, and a screenshot of the problem and we'll get back to you faster.

Requirements

  • iOS 18.0 or later
  • iPhone and iPad
  • Signing in to iCloud is required for syncing and shared ledgers. You can record without it — your data just won't sync across devices.

Where is my data, and can I lose it?

Your records live in your own private iCloud space, not on our servers — we don't have any. A copy is also kept on your device, so you can record without a connection.

See the Privacy Policy.

I got a new phone. How do my records come back?

  1. Sign in to iCloud on the new phone with the same Apple ID;
  2. Install Qianey from the App Store and open it;
  3. Your data syncs down automatically. With a lot of records the first sync takes a while — stay connected.

The data isn't in our hands, so we can't migrate it for you. As long as you keep the same Apple ID, it comes back on its own.

How do I share a ledger with my family?

Find the sharing entry in the ledger settings and send an invitation. The other person needs to:

  • Be signed in to their own Apple ID and iCloud;
  • Open the invitation you sent and accept it.

Everyone records into the same book: who spent what, who fronted the money, who owes whom on a split — all accounted for. To leave a shared ledger, choose leave in the ledger settings; your own data stays yours.

How do I delete my data?

  • Some of it: delete the transactions, ledgers, or attachments in the app.
  • All of it: delete the app, then remove Qianey's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

To keep a copy first, export your records as CSV or JSON any time (ledger settings → Export).

I forgot my App Lock, or Face ID won't recognise me

The App Lock setting is stored locally on the device; your records are in iCloud. So:

  1. Delete the app;
  2. Reinstall it;
  3. Sign in with the same Apple ID — your records sync back, App Lock returns to off, and you can set it up again.

Nobody loses their own records because of a forgotten lock.

How do I start budgeting?

Don't try to fill in every category up front. The right way is to record first:

  1. Record normally for a month or two;
  2. Open the budget page: spending that isn't budgeted yet is listed separately, showing where the money actually goes;
  3. Then decide what to cap and at what amount.

Set a total on a parent category and you can split it into sub-limits.

Can I record in a foreign currency?

Yes. When a transaction's currency differs from your ledger's base currency, the app fetches the rate to convert it. Because that needs a network call, it is the app's only external request (see the Privacy Policy).

Can I export? In what format?

Yes, any time — as CSV or JSON, to keep, back up, or process elsewhere.

My two devices disagree / syncing is slow

  • Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID;
  • Check that Qianey is switched on under Settings → your name → iCloud;
  • Stay connected — a first sync with a lot of records takes a few minutes;
  • Pull to refresh, or restart the app and look again.

If they still disagree, email us with a screenshot and both device models and OS versions.
